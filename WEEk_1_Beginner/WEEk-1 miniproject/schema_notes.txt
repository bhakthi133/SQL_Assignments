The database is divided into four tables: customers, orders, products and order_items.

The customers table stores customer details separately using customer_id as the Primary Key.

The orders table stores order details and uses customer_id as a Foreign Key to connect each order with a customer.

The products table stores product details separately using product_id as the Primary Key.

The order_items table connects orders and products using order_id and product_id as Foreign Keys.

Repeating product details in orders are avoided by storing individual products and their quantities in order_items.

This design avoids duplicate customer and product information and reduces insertion, update and deletion anomalies.

The tables follow 3NF by keeping attributes in their respective tables and avoiding partial and transitive dependencies.
